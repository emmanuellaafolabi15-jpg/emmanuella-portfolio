About Me

Emmanuella Olamiposi Afolabi – also known as Elle – is a Product Designer, Creative Writer, and Brand Storyteller from Nigeria.

She is the founder and creative director of Studio Elle, a creative studio dedicated to helping founders, small businesses, and creative professionals grow through thoughtful product design, compelling storytelling, and strategic brand identity.
